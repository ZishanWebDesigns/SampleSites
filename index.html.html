Questions people ask before their first visit

Do I need to know how to use the equipment?

No. Your free walkthrough covers exactly that — a trainer shows you how each machine works and what it's for, at whatever pace you need.

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable moving in. Sneakers and clothes you don't mind sweating in are the only real requirements.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No. The free trial has zero obligation, and every membership can be cancelled anytime with no fees.

What if I'm out of shape?

That's who this gym is built for. Classes are capped at 12 people specifically so instructors can adjust pace for wherever you're starting from.

Can I bring a friend to my free trial?

Yes — bring one guest along at no extra cost. Just mention it when you book.

Ready to see it for yourself?
